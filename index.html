Spin Coating -- Meyerhofer Model

Input
How E away from the reference speed above is estimated for the plot. Meyerhofer measured E ∝ sqrt(rpm) for spinning photoresist; that turns the underlying h ∝ rpm⁻²ₛ³ into the h ∝ rpm⁻¹⁵² usually reported. The point at the reference speed itself is unaffected.
Meyerhofer (1978) assumes viscosity and evaporation rate stay constant while the film dries. Flack et al. (1984) and Bornside et al. (1989) showed both actually depend on the local solute concentration -- viscosity climbs, evaporation slows -- which Advanced lets you switch on below (as a depth-averaged approximation of their full spatial models; see README for what that leaves out).
How the initial solute volume fraction c0 is obtained -- typed directly, or computed from a weight fraction plus both pure-component densities.
w and both densities are converted to a volume fraction assuming ideal (additive) mixing volumes -- the same assumption the Meyerhofer derivation itself makes.
Resin Presets
Presets store material properties (viscosity, density, evaporation rate, solids fraction) -- not the spin speed, since that is a per-run process choice, not a material property. Saved in this browser (localStorage), not shared with anyone else.
